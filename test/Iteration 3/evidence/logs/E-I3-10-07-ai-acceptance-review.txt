Confirmed implemented:
- Bot uses the shared battle engine and returns an action identifier only.
- Legal actions enforce unlocked abilities and Energy costs.
- One standard difficulty is used without hidden stat advantages.
- Bot battles award zero leaderboard points.
- Offline self-play uses +1/-1 outcomes; committed policy records 1,500 completed training matches.
- Evaluation supports random and simple opponents and reports wins, length, actions, habitat and card rates.

Open acceptance gaps:
- AC8.7.2: the state key contains AI Energy but not the child’s Energy; threat scoring uses the child’s Basic Attack and does not model available child abilities or next-action threat.
- AC8.7.9: generated matches vary cards, habitats, unlock tiers, initiative and opponent profile, but start at full HP and 5 Energy; low/full HP and low/high Energy coverage is neither constructed nor asserted.
- AC8.7.10: metrics are printed, but a retained review of ability concentration and persistent card/habitat outliers with adequate samples is absent.
- AC8.7.11: no completed pre-release review artefact shows how consistent imbalance was investigated or corrected. The plan records an 84.4% AI win rate against random actions under the limited standard-start evaluation.
